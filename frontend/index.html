How it works

Three steps, from report to understanding.

1. Upload

Add your report as a PDF or paste the text directly. Scanned reports work too.

2. Summarize

An AI model trained on medical reports writes a plain-language summary, checked for accuracy.

3. Understand in Marathi

Read the Marathi translation, listen to it out loud, or ask a follow-up question by voice.

MedSetu — an accessibility tool, not a diagnostic one. Built as an academic project.
